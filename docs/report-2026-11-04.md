# Ridge Racer 6 port: report for 06:00 AEST, 2026-11-04

The night's work in one place; the running notes are `docs/rr6-notes.md` and `docs/progress-log.md`, the shared
write-up for the other port sessions is the "Making Renut's renderer more accurate: Ridge Racer 6" doc.

## What works

**PC (Direct3D 12, Vulkan).** The game boots, installs its DLC, plays its menus, garage, car select, World Xplorer and
Single Race; four courses (Harborline 765, Lakeshore Drive, Rave City Riverfront at night, and the harbour with the
container cranes) were compared frame against frame with the SDK's emulated Xbox 360 GPU and match: lighting, foliage,
billboards, the car's paint and glass, the HUD. The reflections fix (`rr6_user_clip_plane`), the bloom fix
(`rr6_fetch_over_pointer`), the strip-marker fix (`rr6_strip_markers`), the gamma ramp and the alpha-test handling are
all on by default and A/B-switchable in `renut_ab.toml`.

**Emulator (Ryujinx, deko3d).** Boots and races; the wedges, streaks and torn meshes of the first console run are gone
(the strip-marker fix, confirmed on the console's second run too). The scripted route reaches the race in about one
run of three under the other sessions' load (the main menu appears 100-120 s after start depending on load), which is
the emulator's timing, not the port's.

**Console (the user's runs).** Package 2026-11-03_2203 raced at 42-60 fps handheld (35 docked at 1.5x), menus at 61.
The visual faults that remain are console-only: the chrome car with hard black and white reflections, the bridge
cables as black ribbons, hard rectangles in the sky, noise blocks on the palms and the overpass, the tower's video
screen black. The game's own constants for the car's paint are the emulator's on the console (`[paint]` lines), so the
fault is on the GPU side.

**Packages.** `D:\ridge racer 6\builds\2026-11-04_0309_texture_dump_all_tests12` is the one to use (NRO D97F8203E4B9):
the shipped tomls, `renut_play.toml`, `renut_ab.toml`, and `tests/` with console tests 1-12. The 0110 and 0242 packages
carry notes pointing at it.

## Measured

- Console handheld 1280x720 at 1x: 42-60 fps in the race; docked 1.5x to 1080p: about 35 fps, GPU-bound at 460.8 MHz
  (render thread 2.4-6.4 ms a frame behind it); menus 61 fps; the course intro's lows 7.6 fps; the longest hitch
  536 ms; first boot's DLC install 3 min 46 s.
- Console CPU: 199 % of 300 % with the 1 kHz sampler on: Main 64 % (60 % of it the wait spin), an XThread 53 %, Audio
  Worker 48 % (86 % of it in three functions: `822A8AE0`, `822A6630`, `822BEF40`), the render thread 22 %.
- Emulator: 27-35 fps in the race with every barrier a full wait (test 8); normal settings race at the emulator's cap.
- PC: the race at the 60 fps cap, render thread 0.5 ms a frame, GPU 0.3 ms a frame.
- The texture dump: 122 files (13 MB) a window at the car select written within 1.5 s by the writer thread; a
  whole-frame window of 150 textures about 40 MB.

## Visual issues

**Found and fixed (this stretch).** Strip restart markers counted as vertex 65535 in the range path, which on deko3d
alone (D3D12 cuts at 0xFFFF for any index width) drew huge black wedges and streaks through the course intro and tore
meshes in the race: `rr6_strip_markers`, A/B 77; zero bad frames in 364 emulator bursts after it; gone on the console's
second run. Shared with the other sessions; sasrt-nx took it.

**Left, console-only, with the means to find them now in the package.** The chrome car, the black cable ribbons, the
sky rectangles, the noise blocks and the black tower screen. What was established tonight by reading and by the
emulator: the game's paint constants are right on the console; the upload ring's CPU-cache cleaning is complete before
every submit; the store cards that tell the renderer of guest writes are marked alike on both platforms; deko3d's copy
checks concern block formats, not hardware compression. Three classes remain, with a console test for each: GPU
ordering (tests 6-8), what the hardware does to memory that Ryujinx does not model (hardware colour compression, test
10), and the compiled shaders (an unwritten input or register reads zero in Ryujinx and garbage on the hardware: test
11, every shader optimisation off). And the readback: test 9 copies the car's paint textures (every level, every face)
out of the console's GPU, test 12 every texture a frame samples; `tools/texture_dump_view.py` pictures them and
compares them with the references made tonight, which are byte-identical between Direct3D 12 and deko3d for every
static texture of a Harborline frame. A console texture with no equal twin is the fault, named.

**Caveat on the reference.** The SDK's `anisotropic_override` defaults to 3 (4:1 forced), so the reference captures
were sharper at grazing angles than the game asks (from the Viva Piñata session). A re-record at the game's anisotropy
is in `out/pc_runs/aniso_ref` against `aniso_ours`: PENDING.
